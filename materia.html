The art style of Internet, Please

Materia

A material-first, minimal design system for small strategy games: a calm paper map, bold simple type, and a handful of physical materials you can almost touch. Every surface is made of something, sits in space and is lit by one sun.

Principles

Five rules hold the whole look together.

1

Material first

Before choosing a colour, choose a material. Colour follows from what a surface is made of.

2

Felt, not seen

Grain, strands and brushing at a whisper. If you notice the texture before the shape, it is too strong.

3

One sun

Light comes from the top-left. Every shadow falls down-right, every rim is lit top-left.

4

Calm map, loud lines

The ground stays quiet. The line colours carry all the saturation.

5

Minimal chrome

No borders where a shadow can separate. No decoration that is not a material.

Six materials, one sun

Every surface is made of something.

Drag the sun and watch the shadows follow. Let go, and it goes home to the top-left, where it always lives.

Colour

Paper by day. Slate by dusk.

Each token comes in two themes. Dusk lifts the line colours a little and deepens every shadow: the sun is lower. Tap a swatch to copy its Day value.

Two themes

Same town, lower sun.

Slide between Day and Dusk. The dusk set gives every outline a light ink so it reads on slate.

Type

One family. Three weights.

Everything is set in Outfit at 500, 700 and 800. Numbers are heroes: scores and days use tabular figures, with a small uppercase label above. Label is the only uppercase style.

Shape

Corners are squircles, not arcs.

A continuous curve, like an app icon. Square tiles round off at 22.37% of their side. Line tokens, stations and switches stay perfectly round.

Squircle (solid) against a circular-arc corner (dashed). The curve starts earlier and ends softer.

1 2 chip Play

Buttons 14, cards 22, panels 32, chips 6. Tiles at 22.37%.

Light and shadow

Higher things cast longer shadows.

Shadows are cool blue-grey, like skylight on a sunny day, never pure black. Hover lifts toward the sun; press sinks into the ground.

Quick and springy: 140 to 200 ms. Things lift, drop and settle. Nothing spins or bounces more than once.

Iconography

Two-pixel strokes. One soft tint.

A 24 pixel grid, round caps and joins, and a fill at 18%. A few icons carry a tiny face: the homes, the sun and the moon. Utility icons never do.

Voice

Short, friendly, sentence case.

A panel has one title and at most two sentences. Speak to the player as you. The icons carry the joy.

Router

A fast hub with few ports. Built for heavy traffic.

Put one between the Internet and a group of homes.

  • Do: "Drag from the Internet to a home to bring it online."
  • Don't: exclamation marks, emoji, jargon, or white text on a line colour.
