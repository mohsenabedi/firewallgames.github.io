DW/08 — Questions & answers

Deadwave FAQ

Q/01What type of zombie game is Deadwave?

Deadwave is a fast-paced arcade zombie game for PC combining round-based co-op zombie survival, competitive PvP modes (Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, Prop Hunt), and stylized zombie waves — built in Unreal Engine 5 by Firewall Games.

Q/02Is Deadwave a good zombie game for kids?

Yes. Deadwave uses stylized, cartoon-inspired zombie visuals without realistic gore, making it a family-friendly zombie game for kids ages 10 and up. The arcade-style gameplay is easy to pick up and fun to play co-op with parents or friends.

Q/03Is Deadwave a multiplayer zombie game?

Yes. Deadwave is a multiplayer zombie game for PC supporting up to 12 players in PvP lobbies and 1–4 players in co-op zombie survival, powered by Epic Online Services for cross-platform matchmaking.

Q/04Does Deadwave have a co-op mode?

Yes. Deadwave's co-op zombie survival mode pits 1–4 players against 20 escalating rounds of zombie waves with boss encounters every 5th round, shared life pools, team revives, and arcade-style power pickups including Nukes and Air-Strikes.

Q/05Where can I play Deadwave?

Deadwave is available on PC via Steam. Wishlist it at store.steampowered.com to be notified when Early Access goes live.

Deadwave — Arcade Zombie Game on Steam

Deadwave is an indie arcade zombie game developed and published by Firewall Games, available on Steam for PC (Windows). The game releases in June 2026. A free playable demo is available on Steam. The full game is priced at $14.99 USD.

Deadwave is built in Unreal Engine 5 and combines co-op zombie survival (1–4 players, 20 escalating rounds, boss waves, shared life pool, arcade power pickups like Nukes and Air-Strikes) with competitive PvP modes including Team Deathmatch, Capture the Flag, and Prop Hunt — supporting up to 12 players via Epic Online Services cross-platform matchmaking.

The game features stylized, cartoon-inspired zombie visuals with no realistic gore, making it suitable for kids ages 10 and up, teens, and adult zombie game fans. It is fast-paced, easy to pick up, and designed for both controller and mouse+keyboard.
