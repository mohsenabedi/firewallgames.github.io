Studio bio

Firewall Games delivers stylized, Unreal Engine 5 signal craft.

Firewall Games is a micro-studio weaving synthwave atmospheres, surreal horror, and improvisational mechanics into new games players can stream, speedrun, and survive.

  • Atmospheric stylized design that hums with neon static and sci-fi horror.
  • Advanced mechanics tuned for tension, improvisation, and co-op zombies play.
  • Signal-bent art direction rooted in Unreal Engine 5 pipelines.
